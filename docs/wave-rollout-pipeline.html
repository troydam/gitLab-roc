Wave Rollout Pipeline

How a change moves from dev to prod: three environments, three tiers per environment, three rollout waves per tier. Every amber gate below waits for a person to click Play in GitLab — the only job that runs on its own is lint.

Job reference

Every job below shares one .deploy-template and differs only in ENVIRONMENT, PHASE, and WAVE. All 27 deploy jobs are when: manual.

Gating pattern per wave — applies identically to db, app, and web in every environment
Wave Job pattern Runs when Targets
canary <env>:<tier>:canary manual — clickable once the previous tier's final passes (or, for the first job in dev, once lint passes) <env>_<tier>_canary
wave2 <env>:<tier>:wave2 manual after canary is reviewed <env>_<tier>_wave2
final <env>:<tier>:final manual after wave2 is reviewed <env>_<tier>_final
promotion gate preprod:db:canary, prod:db:canary manual — mechanically the same rule as every other canary, but this click also promotes the whole environment (dev→preprod, preprod→prod) preprod_db_canary, prod_db_canary
